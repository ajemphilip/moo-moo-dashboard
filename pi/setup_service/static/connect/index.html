Connect to your Moo

Use Bluetooth without leaving your current wifi, or use Moo's temporary wifi network.

Bluetooth — stay on your wifi

Best for laptop setup. Chrome or Edge will ask you to choose a nearby Moo.

or

Temporary Moo wifi

The original setup method remains available.

  1. 1Open this device's Wi-Fi settings.
  2. 2Choose the network starting with moo-.
  3. 3Return here after it connects.
or

It is already on my wifi

Open the Moo at its address. On iPhones, iPads and Macs moo.local works; other devices may need the number, such as 192.168.2.52. This device has to be on the same wifi as the Moo.
