Story time

Moo tells a story together with your child.

Moo says hello and asks what kind of story to tell. After a chime, your child answers out loud.

Every part of the story has one missing word. Moo reads up to the gap and plays a chime — that's the signal for your child to say a word out loud. Their word drops straight into the story, and Moo reads on with it.

That word is then spelled on the Braille cell. Tap Continue and the story grows from the word your child chose.
